06 · faq

%man beta

is this financial advice?

No. BETA shows which coins are riding a runner's wave and how it would trade them. It never tells you to buy anything, and it never will.

what is a beta?

A coin that moves with a runner: the same name or ticker, the same theme, the same image style or the same dev wallet. When the runner breaks out, the money that missed it goes there next.

what counts as a breakout?

A coin graduating off the pump.fun bonding curve. BETA listens to the pump.fun stream and starts mapping the second the graduation lands.

how are the three picked?

Every beta gets a score from three things: how closely it's related to the runner, how much money is flowing into it right now, and how much headroom it has below the runner's market cap. Coins too new, too thin or already bigger than the runner are skipped.

are the buys real?

Not yet. The breakouts, the beta map and the prices are live pump.fun and DexScreener data. The vault's buys are paper fills at real prices while the engine is in beta, and every basket on this page is marked sim.

what can't it do?

Betas are copies. Many rug and most fade with the runner. A high score means related and moving, not safe. The dev lane only sees coins launched while the page is open, and the look lane needs both coins to have an image.

will beta DM me?

Never. Anyone who does is not us.
